Interpretation: There may be something worth reading at that post.

Observable signal: Ears hanging forward, tail swinging, short quick steps.

Private POV: Oh, that one. I know that smell. Park in a moment—there is something here.

## Dog response 6

Memory update: Picked up the ball and lifted it clear of the snow. Its familiar texture is cold and wet in my mouth; Human watches across the grass.

Slush had pressed between his toes on the pavement, but the lamppost had held his attention: a playmate’s scent, strong at its base, trailing towards the park. Then the lead came off and Human brought out the ball. He bowed, watched it fly, and tore straight through the puddle. Cold water splashed his belly. Now he held it clear of the trampled snow, familiar against his jaws, meltwater cold on his tongue. Across the grass stood Human.
