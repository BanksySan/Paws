# Chapter 1 — Cat begins her evening patrol


1. Cat is looking out of the back door. She takes in her world: the garden, the snow, the cold wet slush. She needs to go outside, but she isn't keen on the cold, and she knows there is open ground between her and the safety of the fence.
2. She has her set routes. Out and to the right, quickly across the open ground, then straight onto the fence. From the fence she can see both her garden and the neighbour's garden.
3. The fence gives her options. If the neighbour's garden is clear, she can follow it towards the tree in the far corner. If there is trouble, she can drop back into her own garden and use another route.
4. She sniffs the air and listens carefully, preparing to commit only once she's satisfied that the coast is clear.
5. There's a gentle push from behind her. One of her humans. They do that sometimes. They don't seem to understand that standing in the doorway is part of going outside.
6. Cat has no choice now. She quickly traverses the open ground to the fence. Her claws make the vertical climb easy, and she moves along the narrow top as comfortably as if it were flat ground.
7. The neighbour's garden is quiet, so she follows the fence towards the tree and climbs.
8. She pauses amongst the branches, taking in the view of her own garden and the neighbour's garden before continuing.
9. She follows one of her familiar patrol routes, crossing the boundaries between neighbouring gardens until she reaches the roof of a lean-to, where she stops again.
10. Here she listens.
    1. What has changed since she was last here?
    2. What sounds are there?
    3. What is moving?
    4. What is not moving that should be?
11. Another cat approaches. Cat knows this one. It is pushy, territorial, and inclined to test whether a warning really means what it says.
12. Cat stays where she is and assesses the situation. There is no reason for a fight if the other cat has enough sense to leave things alone.
13. The other cat comes closer. Cat lets out a low growl. The very tip of her tail moves, almost imperceptibly.
14. Neither cat particularly wants a violent confrontation, but neither can simply ignore the other. They have to establish what is going to happen.
15. They stare at one another, almost frozen.
    1. Cat does not move.
    2. The other cat does not move.
    3. Cat's tail-tip twitches again.
    4. The other cat's tail moves slightly too.
    5. Each watches for the smallest change in posture or intention.
16. The other cat walks closer, testing her.
    1. Cat growls louder.
    2. The other cat responds.
    3. Cat rises higher on her legs and arches her back.
    4. Her tail is now twitching visibly.
17. The other cat presses its luck again.
    1. Cat's tail begins to flick sharply from side to side.
    2. She hisses — loud and unmistakable.
    3. The other cat still doesn't retreat.
    4. Enough is enough. Cat has made herself perfectly clear.
    5. With a roar — by Cat's standards — she launches forward, paws and claws ready.
18. That is enough. The other cat backs down and runs.
19. Cat stops rather than pursuing. The point has been made. She settles again, watching for a moment but, more importantly, listening.
20. The sun is getting lower. This does not bother her. The world is not disappearing; it is changing. Colours fade, contrasts sharpen, shadows deepen, and movement becomes more important.
21. She continues her patrol, carefully and deliberately.
22. As the evening deepens, the gardens become busier in ways the humans would barely notice. Cat hears tiny movements beneath hedges and leaves, wings settling into branches, faint scratching and rustling from things beginning their own evening business.
23. Her ears twitch and turn towards each sound. She can locate movement without moving the rest of her body at all.
24. Something small moves beneath the hedge.
25. Cat changes instantly. She lowers herself close to the ground and becomes almost completely silent. Her attention fixes on the movement. Her ears turn towards every faint scrape and rustle; each paw is placed slowly and precisely. Nothing else matters.
26. A small furry shape appears between the dead leaves. Mouse, vole — Cat neither knows nor cares. It is small, it is moving, and it is prey.
27. Cat watches, absolutely still. Only her ears move. When the little animal moves, she moves: one careful step, then another, her body low and fluid, a tiny panther in the gathering dark.
28. The prey stops. Cat stops with it.
29. For several moments neither appears to move at all. Cat listens to everything around her while remaining utterly focused on the small shape ahead.
30. It moves again. Cat advances, silent and deliberate, closing the distance.
31. Suddenly it darts away.
32. Cat explodes from stillness into speed, racing after it.
33. The little animal reaches the shed first and vanishes beneath the narrow gap at its base.
34. Cat arrives a fraction too late. She crouches beside the gap, staring into the darkness beneath the shed and listening.
35. Nothing.
36. Cat waits. It may come out again.
37. By now the last daylight has gone. The darkness does not trouble her, but standing still does. The cold begins to work through her short coat and up through her paws.
38. Then she hears a familiar call from home. One of her humans is calling her.
39. Cat looks once more beneath the shed. Whatever is hiding there can stay there.
40. There is a warm house waiting.
41. She turns for home.
42. The human is standing at the door, waiting for her.
43. Cat stands at the door for a while, considering the room inside. It's warm and nothing awful has ever happened there, but caution is still important.
44. The human makes some sound and starts closing the door, but Cat won't be rushed.
45. Eventually, after the human has closed and then opened the door again several times, Cat is confident that it's safe to enter.
46. Cat walks casually in and shakes some snow off her fur and paws.
47. Cat heads straight for the warmth of the human's bedroom and starts cleaning herself.
