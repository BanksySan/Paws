# Chapter 4 — Cat on the Bed — Cat's perspective

## Warmth at last

1. Cat is curled comfortably on the bed.
    1. She is warm.
    2. She is dry, at last, after an evening of snow, slush and cold paws.
    3. She has cleaned her coat and settled into the warm bedding.
    4. One of her humans had been quietly in the room earlier, but has since gone elsewhere. The room is peaceful now.
    5. The cold garden is somebody else's problem. This is considerably better.
2. She hears the front door open downstairs.
    1. A familiar Human calls into the house.
    2. Another Human answers.
    3. Cat hears all this without needing to lift her head.
3. Then she hears Dog.
    1. A faint jingle of metal.
    2. Heavy paws on the hall floor.
    3. Excited movement and breathing.
    4. Dog has returned from his walk.
4. Cat stays where she is.
    1. Dog is downstairs.
    2. Cat is upstairs.
    3. There are several walls, a ceiling and a flight of stairs between them.
    4. This arrangement is entirely satisfactory.

## Unwelcome sounds downstairs

5. The hallway becomes busy.
    1. Big Human moves about and talks to Dog.
    2. Dog moves about considerably more.
    3. Cat hears the rough sound of a towel passing through wet fur.
    4. Wet paws scrape and shift as Big Human tries to attend to them.
    5. Dog appears unwilling to remain in one place indefinitely.
6. Cat's ears turn towards the stairs.
    1. The sounds below are familiar.
    2. They are also reassuringly far away.
    3. She returns her attention to the warmth of the bed.
7. Something changes.
    1. A sudden scramble downstairs.
    2. Big Human calls sharply.
    3. Heavy paws strike the bottom stair.
    4. Then the next.
8. Dog is coming upstairs. Fast.
    1. Cat lifts her head.
    2. The pawbeats come closer in rapid succession.
    3. Big Human's footsteps and voice follow some distance behind.
    4. Cat has barely enough time to locate the noise before Dog reaches the bedroom.

## The bed is invaded

9. Dog bursts into the room.
    1. Large.
    2. Wet.
    3. Moving fast.
    4. His paws strike the floor with heavy thumps.
    5. His own tail sweeps wildly behind him.
10. Dog heads straight for the bed.
    1. Cat cannot tell what he intends to do next.
    2. She can tell that he is not approaching quietly.
    3. The warm bed has suddenly become a place that requires attention.
11. Dog jumps up.
    1. The whole mattress shifts beneath Cat.
    2. Damp paws land close to her.
    3. Water, melting snow and the remains of the park scatter across the covers.
    4. The bedding Cat has spent the evening getting warm and dry is no longer entirely either.
12. Dog pushes his large head towards her.
    1. His nose is getting closer.
    2. There is a great deal of Dog attached to that nose.
    3. He is excited, noisy and not showing any sign of stopping.
13. Cat does not wait to discover whether he can stop safely.
    1. Staying would put her within reach of his paws and body.
    2. She chooses distance instead.
    3. She bolts from the bed.

## The windowsill

14. Cat crosses the room and springs onto the windowsill.
    1. Height gives her space away from Dog.
    2. From here she can watch him and decide what to do next.
    3. The sill is narrow, however. The window is closed, and the room is the only way out.
    4. She turns to face Dog.
15. Dog follows at once.
    1. He jumps down and crosses the room.
    2. Cat has left the bed; Dog comes after her anyway.
    3. She has already made her preference clear, as far as she is concerned.
16. Cat's tail-tip twitches.
    1. She watches the approaching body.
    2. Her ears begin to turn backwards.
    3. The tip of her tail flicks more sharply.
    4. Dog continues looking at her with unwelcome interest.
17. Cat gives her first audible warning.
    1. A low growl begins in her throat.
    2. She hisses sharply.
    3. Dog stops for a moment.
    4. Good. Perhaps he has finally understood that she wants him farther away.
18. Dog comes closer again.
    1. He rises onto his back legs.
    2. His front paws come up towards the sill.
    3. His chest and shoulders fill the space below Cat.
    4. His nose rises towards her.
    5. His tail continues moving as though this is all an enjoyable business.
19. Cat's situation has changed.
    1. The sill gave her useful distance while Dog stayed on the floor.
    2. Now he has brought his head up to her level.
    3. The route back across the room passes very close to him.
    4. She has options, but none that create the distance she needs without exposing her to his body.
20. Cat gives a second, unmistakable warning.
    1. Her ears flatten.
    2. Her tail lashes from side to side.
    3. She hisses again, louder.
    4. Dog stops.
    5. For a moment, Cat expects him to withdraw.
21. Dog moves his head towards her yet again.
    1. His nose comes closer.
    2. She has warned him twice, and he has continued both times.
    3. Now the immediate problem is the great wet head entering the little space she has left.

## One paw is enough

22. Cat strikes.
    1. A single, astonishingly fast movement.
    2. One paw, claws extended.
    3. A precise blow aimed at making Dog move away, not an attempt to pursue or fight him.
    4. Cat is capable of considerably greater ferocity when the situation demands it. Here, she needs distance, not a battle.
23. One claw catches Dog across the nose.
24. Dog yelps and springs backwards.
    1. His paws return to the floor.
    2. He backs away from the windowsill.
    3. Finally, there is room between them.
25. Cat stays on the windowsill.
    1. Her ears remain back and her tail still lashes.
    2. She watches Dog closely in case he tries again.
    3. He does not.
26. Dog stares at Cat. Cat stares back.
    1. She moved away from him on the bed.
    2. He followed her across the room.
    3. She warned him.
    4. He kept coming.
    5. She warned him again.
    6. He stopped — and then moved closer anyway.
    7. What exactly did he expect?
27. Cat does not follow or strike again.
    1. Dog is keeping his distance now.
    2. The problem has been solved as far as Cat is concerned.
    3. She remains alert, though. Dog has a habit of starting things again.

## The humans arrive

28. Big Human reaches the bedroom, and Little Human arrives soon after.
    1. They have heard Dog's yelp.
    2. Big Human goes to Dog and gently checks his nose.
    3. He notices the wet paw marks and disturbed bedding.
    4. Cat stays on her sill. She has no wish to join the bustle.
29. Little Human sees Cat and starts towards her.
    1. Little Human likes Cat and seems to want to comfort her.
    2. Cat likes Little Human too, but she can be rather sudden in her movements.
    3. Little Human sometimes grabs for Cat and holds her rather more firmly than Cat wants.
    4. Cat has only just made Dog keep his distance; being grabbed now would not be an improvement.
    5. Cat watches her approach carefully.
30. Big Human notices and gently calls or signals to Little Human.
    1. Little Human stops before reaching Cat.
    2. Big Human encourages her to give Cat some space.
    3. Little Human backs away from the windowsill.
    4. Good. Big Human understands something important.
31. Little Human turns to Dog.
    1. She checks his nose and gives him a big hug.
    2. Dog leans into her affection.
    3. His tail moves; he seems to like being fussed over in this way.
    4. Cat cannot see what is so appealing about having someone wrap their arms around you just now, but Dog plainly does.
32. Big Human gently asks Little Human to take Dog downstairs.
    1. Little Human encourages Dog towards the door.
    2. Dog goes willingly enough, though he glances back at Cat.
    3. Cat watches until his large body disappears down the stairs.
    4. The room is quieter again. The bed, unfortunately, is still wet.

## The right kind of company

33. Big Human stays with Cat.
    1. He doesn't hurry towards her or reach suddenly.
    2. Cat knows him well. His movements are familiar and predictable.
    3. She remains on the sill, watching, but she does not feel the need to escape.
34. Big Human approaches calmly.
    1. He speaks softly and offers his hand.
    2. Cat has the opportunity to look, smell and decide.
    3. She accepts his approach.
35. Big Human gently picks Cat up.
    1. His hands support her securely.
    2. She allows herself to be held.
    3. He strokes her with the familiar gentle pressure she enjoys.
36. Cat begins to relax.
    1. Her tail settles.
    2. Her body softens against Big Human.
    3. She starts purring.
37. Cat rubs her face against Big Human.
    1. One cheek, then the other.
    2. He is warm, familiar and safe.
    3. The rubbing is affection, but it also leaves a little of Cat's scent on him.
    4. Big Human smells a little more like Cat now.
    5. This is much better.
