# Chapter 4 — Cat on the Bed — Cat's perspective


1. Cat is curled comfortably on the bed.
    1. She is warm.
    2. She is dry.
    3. The cold garden is now somebody else's problem.
    4. This is considerably better.
2. She hears the front door open downstairs.
3. Human calls into the house.
4. Another Human answers.
5. Then Cat hears something else.
    1. The jingle of metal.
    2. Wet paws moving on the floor.
    3. Heavy breathing.
    4. Dog.
6. Cat remains where she is.
    1. Dog has returned from his walk.
    2. He is downstairs.
    3. Cat is upstairs.
    4. This arrangement is satisfactory.
7. There is activity in the hallway below.
    1. Human moving about.
    2. Dog moving about considerably more.
    3. The rough sound of a towel rubbing through wet fur.
    4. Human talking to Dog.
    5. Dog refusing to remain entirely where Human wants him.
8. Cat's ears turn towards the stairs.
9. Something changes below.
    1. A sudden scramble.
    2. Human calls sharply.
    3. Dog's paws hit the stairs.
10. Dog is coming upstairs.
11. Fast.
12. Cat lifts her head.
    1. The footsteps come closer.
    2. Heavy pawbeats, one after another.
    3. Dog is not slowing down.
13. Dog bursts into the bedroom.
14. He comes straight towards the bed.
    1. Large.
    2. Wet.
    3. Moving quickly.
    4. His paws hit the floor heavily.
    5. His tail is sweeping wildly behind him.
15. Dog jumps onto the bed.
16. The whole mattress moves beneath Cat.
    1. Wet paws land close to her.
    2. Water and melting snow scatter across the bedding.
    3. Dog's large head comes directly towards her.
    4. His nose is coming closer.
17. Cat does not wait to discover whether Dog intends to stop.
18. She bolts.
19. Cat crosses the room and jumps onto the windowsill.
    1. Height gives her distance.
    2. The narrow sill gives Dog less room to approach.
    3. But now she has no easy exit.
    4. She turns to face him.
20. Dog follows her.
21. Of course he follows her.
22. Cat's tail begins to twitch.
    1. She has moved away.
    2. Dog has followed.
    3. That ought to be enough information.
23. Dog crosses the room towards her.
    1. Cat's tail flicks more sharply.
    2. She hisses loudly.
    3. Dog does not take the hint.
24. Dog continues towards her.
    1. He rises onto his back legs.
    2. He puts his front paws up towards the windowsill.
    3. His body fills the space below her.
    4. His head rises towards hers.
    5. His nose comes closer.
    6. His tail is still sweeping energetically behind him.
25. Cat's tail flicks harder.
26. Dog keeps coming.
27. Cat hisses again.
28. The message is unmistakable.
29. Dog stops.
30. For a moment, Cat thinks he has finally understood.
31. Then Dog moves his head towards her again.
32. Cat's tail lashes sharply.
33. She has nowhere further to retreat without abandoning the windowsill and putting herself back on the floor beside Dog.
34. Dog's nose comes closer.
35. Cat strikes.
    1. Fast.
    2. One paw.
    3. Claws out.
    4. A single blow intended to make Dog move away.
    5. For Cat, this is not a brawl; this is a jab.
    6. If Cat wanted to do serious damage, she is quite capable of doing so. A real Cat attack is a serious thing.
36. Her claw catches Dog across the nose.
37. Dog yelps and jumps backwards.
38. Finally, there is distance between them.
39. Cat stays on the windowsill.
    1. Her body remains tense.
    2. Her tail lashes.
    3. She watches Dog carefully in case he approaches again.
40. Dog stares at her.
41. Cat stares back.
    1. She does not understand why this keeps happening.
    2. She moved away.
    3. He followed.
    4. She moved somewhere he could not easily reach.
    5. He followed.
    6. She warned him.
    7. He kept coming.
    8. She warned him again.
    9. He stopped.
    10. Then he came closer anyway.
    11. What exactly did Dog expect?
42. Humans come running up the stairs.
43. One Human goes immediately to Dog.
44. Another notices the wet bed.
45. Cat remains on the windowsill.
    1. Nobody needs to examine her.
    2. Dog did not hurt her.
    3. That was the point.
46. Little Human runs into the room.
47. Little Human examines Dog's nose and gives him a large hug.
48. Cat watches from the windowsill.
49. Dog looks back at her.
50. Little Human starts walking towards Cat.
    1. Little Human can also be unpredictable.
    2. Cat watches carefully.
    3. Big Human says something gently to Little Human.
    4. Little Human stops and backs away.
    5. Good.
51. As far as Cat can tell, Dog charged into a peaceful room, chased her from the bed, ignored her warnings and then seemed astonished when she finally made him stop.
52. One Human takes Dog away.
53. The other Big Human approaches Cat.
    1. Big Humans are predictable.
    2. This Human approaches calmly.
    3. Big Human gently picks her up.
    4. Cat accepts being held.
54. Cat rubs her face against Human.
    1. Human is warm.
    2. Human is familiar.
    3. Human is safe.
    4. Cat rubs her cheeks and face against Human with affection.
    5. Human smells a little more like Cat now.
55. Cat starts purring.
