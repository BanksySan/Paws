# Cat and Dog

A children's story for readers around ten, set close to Christmas. Cat and Dog share a family home but often misunderstand one another. An unexplained body swap allows each to experience the other's world, challenging their assumptions about one another.

**Project stage:** planning and early prose testing. For current coverage, unfinished chapters and open questions, see [Project Status](PROJECT_STATUS.md).

**Working title ideas:** *On the Other Paw* · *Paws for Thought* · *Wrong Paws* (none selected).

## Working with the material

For a writing task, consult the applicable project-wide references, the relevant character bible, the parent act guide and the particular chapter beats. Character bibles contain author knowledge and possible future developments; **a viewpoint character must not automatically know all that material**.

Keep each file within its responsibility. Place scene-specific developments and unresolved alternatives in their relevant act or chapter files, rather than in this README or the canon. Do not silently treat a provisional plan as established fact or let a later document override a conflict without an editorial decision.
